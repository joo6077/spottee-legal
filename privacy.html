2. 보유 기간

  • 계정 정보 · 운동 기록 · 그룹 · 채팅 · 사진: 회원 탈퇴 때까지. 탈퇴를 요청하면 계정이 즉시 비활성화되어 다른 사용자에게 보이지 않으며, 저장된 개인정보는 백업본을 포함해 30일 안에 삭제합니다.
  • 로그인 세션: 접근 토큰 15분, 갱신 토큰 30일. 로그아웃하면 즉시 폐기합니다.
  • 서버 백업: 하루 1회 만들며 7일 뒤 자동 삭제됩니다.
  • 오류 진단 기록: 수집 후 90일.
  • 법령이 보관을 요구하는 기록(예: 전자상거래법 · 통신비밀보호법상 접속 기록)은 그 법이 정한 기간 동안 보관합니다.

Privacy Policy (English summary)

Spottee is a workout-logging and group-training app run by an individual developer. We collect what the app needs to work: account details (email, hashed password, nickname, handle, avatar; or your Google/Apple account identifier and email if you sign in that way), your workout records, outdoor route location only while you choose to track a route, photos/videos you attach, group chats and posts, a push-notification token, and crash reports. We do not show ads, do not use advertising identifiers or behavioural analytics, and never sell personal data.

Processors: Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Sign-In, Gmail for verification mail), Apple (Sign in with Apple), and Sentry (crash reports). Everything else is stored on servers we operate in South Korea. Data is kept until you delete your account; deletion from backups completes within 30 days. Crash reports are kept 90 days. The service is not for children under 14.

You can edit or delete your content in the app and delete your account under Settings → Delete account. For access, correction, deletion or other requests, email joo6077@gmail.com. Effective 2026-10-26.
